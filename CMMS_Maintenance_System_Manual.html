1. Executive Overview & System Concept

The Register-Based Maintenance Management System is specifically engineered to digitally replace physical paper maintenance registers and clipboard slips with a unified, tamper-evident digital architecture.

Facilities traditionally maintain three disconnected paper records:

  1. The Physical Booking Logbook: Located at the front desk or reception, where occupants report defects by hand.
  2. The Maintenance Work Register: Kept by supervisors to track technician job allocations, time taken, and pending spare parts.
  3. The Maintenance Work Order Slip: Carried on clipboards by technicians, hand-signed upon job completion.

This CMMS directly reproduces every column, field, and workflow of those physical registers while adding auto-incremented Booking IDs (#2100+), real-time status transitions, digital stylus/touch signatures, Excel/PDF exports, and live KPI analytics.

3. User Roles & Exact Workflows (What Each User Does)

🏢 Role 1: Administrator admin@cmms.local

The Administrator is the facility coordinator managing complaints, assignments, and ticket closures.

  • Logging Complaints: Navigates to /app/maintenance-booking-register and clicks "New Booking". Enters the reporting person, building, floor, room, and defect description.
  • Automatic Booking ID: The system automatically issues a sequential Booking ID starting at #2100.
  • Dispatching Technicians: Assigns an available technician (e.g. Rajesh Technician). The booking status automatically advances from Pending to Assigned.
  • Monitoring Operations: Monitors the Maintenance Work Register to verify whether jobs are progressing or blocked by pending parts.
  • Supervisory Closure: Once the technician finishes work, the Admin verifies the repair, enters Closing Remarks, assigns an official Closed ID (e.g., CL-2100), and marks the ticket Closed.
👷 Role 2: Maintenance Technician / Worker worker@cmms.local

The Field Technician executes repairs on-site, records operational progress, logs blockers, and signs off.

  • Checking Assigned Jobs: Opens /app/maintenance-work-register to see jobs assigned to their name.
  • Commencing Work: Upon starting the repair, records the Starting Date & Time and updates status to In Progress.
  • Logging Blockers (Pending Reasons): If parts are missing (e.g., waiting for valve or replacement compressor), the technician records the exact reason in the Pending Reason column so facility managers can expedite procurement.
  • Concluding Work: Records the Finishing Date and total Time Taken (e.g. 2 hrs 15 mins), changing status to Completed.
  • Digital Signature: Opens /app/maintenance-work-order, checks the work order row, and signs with a stylus, mouse, or touchscreen on the digital signature canvas.
👑 Role 3: Super Administrator superadmin@test.com

The Super Admin governs multi-tenant instances, campus divisions, user permissions, and database health.

  • Provisions facility divisions and campus accounts across multi-site organizations.
  • Configures security controls, JWT expiration settings, and custom role permissions.
  • Oversees system health, audit logs, and global database operations.
🔍 Role 4: Supervisor Assigned Supervisory Staff

The Supervisor provides independent quality assurance and technical compliance.

  • Performs physical site inspections to confirm repairs meet safety and quality standards.
  • Reviews technician pending logs to approve spare parts requisitions.
  • Co-signs the printable maintenance work order form.
👤 Role 5: Staff / Complaint Reporter Building Occupants & Department Staff

The Complaint Reporter notifies the facility team of issues.

  • Reports defects: location (Building, Floor, Room) and description of the breakdown.
  • Receives the Booking ID to track repair status through completion.

D. Maintenance KPI Dashboard

Route: /app/maintenance-dashboard

High-level command center with seven real-time operational KPI cards and visual summaries:

  • Total Bookings: Total volume of logged complaints.
  • Pending Complaints: Unattended tickets awaiting technician allocation.
  • Assigned Work Orders: Dispatched jobs ready for technician commencement.
  • In Progress: Active on-site maintenance jobs.
  • Completed Work: Finished repairs awaiting supervisor audit and closure.
  • Closed Work Orders: Officially certified and closed tickets.
  • Overdue Maintenance: Tickets exceeding SLA resolution timeframes.
  • Technician & Building Breakdown: Tables displaying technician resolution speed and building defect concentrations.

5. End-to-End 11-Step Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1
Complaint Reporting: Staff notices a defect (e.g. Ground floor water tap leakage) and notifies facility desk.
2
Booking Creation: Admin creates a booking in the Maintenance Booking Register.
3
Booking ID Generated: System generates a unique Booking ID (e.g. 2100).
4
Technician Assignment: Admin assigns the work to a technician. Status changes to Assigned.
5
Work Queue Review: Technician logs in, views assigned work in the Maintenance Work Register.
6
Work Commenced: Technician starts work and enters the Starting Date. Status changes to In Progress.
7
Progress Update: Technician updates work details, materials, and steps taken.
8
Pending Logging (if blocked): If incomplete due to missing parts, technician logs the Pending Reason.
9
Completion & Signing: Technician logs Finishing Date, Time Taken, and signs the Work Order Form. Status updates to Completed.
10
Supervisor Verification: Supervisor verifies completed work, enters Closing Remarks and Closed ID (e.g. CL-2100).
11
Final Closure: Ticket is marked as Closed. Booking Register, Work Register, and KPI Dashboard update automatically.
